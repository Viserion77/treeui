Minor, not patch: the regression fix below is the reason for the release, but it ships alongside four new props (`axisValueFormat`, `showRejections`, `uploadingLabel`, `size` on `TStat`), and added API is a minor by semver.

Fixes a regression in 0.32.0: an anchored panel opened from inside a `TModal` rendered **behind** the dialog, and nothing in it could be clicked.

0.32.0 moved `TPopover`, `TMenu`, `TSelect` and `TDatePicker` panels into a `<Teleport to="body">` so an ancestor's `overflow` could not clip them. Leaving the ancestor's clip also left its **stacking context**: a panel positioned `absolute` inside a modal was painted above the dialog for free, but as a sibling of the modal on `body` it stacks by its own `z-index` — and `--tree-z-dropdown` (1000) sits below `--tree-z-modal` (1300). Measured at 320, 390 and 1440: **0 of 42 days** in a `TDatePicker` inside a `TModal` were reachable by `elementFromPoint`, and a real click hit the dialog behind the panel. In 0.31 the panel overflowed the surface by 18px but was clickable, so this was strictly a regression.

`useAnchoredLayer` now walks the trigger's ancestors, takes the highest `z-index` among the positioned ones, and places the panel one above it. Reading the trigger's ancestors rather than having each layer announce a level: the trigger is still inside the layer, so the answer is already in the DOM, and `TDrawer` — or any layer added later — is covered without either side knowing about the other. The panel's own stylesheet value is a floor, so a trigger inside a card with `z-index: 1` cannot pull a 1000 panel down to 2, and outside any layer the stylesheet keeps deciding.

Verified in a browser, which is the only place it is visible: 3/3 options and 42/42 days reachable at 320×640, 390×844 and 1440×900, with the modal surface still free of overflow, and an unchanged panel outside a modal. The unit test covers the decision rather than the geometry — jsdom has no layout — and it fails against the previous behaviour.

The same release closes the rest of the validation feedback on 0.32.0, plus four items found alongside it.

**`TChart`'s edge anchoring never applied, and the y-axis had no escape at 320px.** The first and last x labels were given a `text-anchor` **attribute**, which CSS always beats — `.t-chart__axis-label--x { text-anchor: middle }` silently won, and the last label kept rendering centred and hanging off the SVG. They are modifier classes now (`is-anchor-start` / `is-anchor-end`). Separately, the space reserved for y labels is capped at 40% of the chart, so `R$ 1.500.000.000,00` clipped at 320px and the consumer had no lever, because one `valueFormat` served both axis and tooltip. **`axisValueFormat`** formats the ticks alone and falls back to `valueFormat`: `R$ 1,5 bi` on the axis, the exact figure in the tooltip.

**`TFileUpload` has no English left, and its refusals follow the language.** `uploadingLabel`, `uploadedAnnouncement` and `failedAnnouncement` cover the spinner and the live region, which the first pass missed — the three props it shipped covered the buttons but not what the screen reader announces during and after an upload. A rejection is also held as **data** now and formatted during render; it used to be stored as its rendered string, frozen at refusal time, so switching language left the old sentence on screen. **`showRejections`** turns the built-in messages off for a product that already surfaces `files-rejected` in its own alert — the event fires either way.

**`TCodeBlock` reserves the copy button's width.** With `copyable` and `wrap` together, a wrapped line ran underneath the button and the characters behind it were unreadable. This only became visible once `wrap` started breaking for real in 0.32.0: before, the long token overflowed sideways and never reached the button.

**`TDonutChart`'s legend keeps the value inside the card.** The label is the flexible column (`flex: 1 1 0`, `min-width: 0`, ellipsis, full text in `title`) and the value is fixed (`flex: 0 0 auto`); a 60-character label used to push the value out.

**`TStat` gains the density axis.** The value's floor was 1.5rem at every size, so a formatted negative currency could not fit two-per-row on a phone and a grid of six indicators collapsed into one tall column. `size="sm"` lowers the floor to 1.125rem and tightens the padding; `lg` completes the axis. The value still scales to its own card through the container query, not to the viewport. Measured at 430px: four tiles two-per-row, every value inside its card at 18px.

**New: `TIconTile`.** The pill behind a `TStat`'s icon, as a primitive. It was drawn inside TStat and reachable only by rendering a whole TStat, so a feature card wanting the same affordance had a bare `TIcon` as its only public route — and a bare icon inherits the text colour, carries a flat 2px stroke and has no ground behind it. At 26px on a phone that reads as a grey scratch rather than a glyph, which is how the report arrived: a photo of three such icons on a dark Android screen with the words "you cannot tell what it is". Recreating the tile in product CSS is what the consumer contract forbids, so a recipe was not an option.

`name`, `tone` (`brand` | `neutral` | `success` | `warning` | `danger` | `info`), `size` (2rem / 2.75rem / 3.5rem, with the glyph stepping 16/20/24px so the ink keeps its proportion), and `label` — which is what decides between `role="img"` with that name and `aria-hidden`, because a glyph beside a heading that already says the same thing is decoration, and announcing it twice is noise. `packages/tokens/src/icon-tile-contrast.test.ts` measures every tone in both themes against **3:1**, the WCAG 1.4.11 floor for a non-text component; a badge clears 4.5:1 because a badge carries a word.

**`TStat` stops being obliged to draw a card, and `TStatGroup` arrives.** A stat has always drawn its own frame and no prop turned it off, so inside a `TCard` — where a measured majority of real usages sit — it is a card within a card: doubled frame, summed padding, and a dashboard of eight indicators reads as eight objects rather than one row of figures. `variant="plain"` keeps the padding, the type and the tones and drops the border, radius, shadow and background.

`TStatGroup` owns the surface and the hairline between cells. Children inside it default to `plain` through a provided context rather than the consumer repeating `variant="plain"` eight times — the kind of repetition that goes wrong on the ninth one added later. The track is a `TGrid` with `gap: 0`, composed rather than re-implemented, so `columns`, `minItemWidth` and `balance` behave as they do everywhere else. The lines are pseudo-elements on each cell with the track pulled back a pixel, so the outer ones are clipped: a cell that reflows onto a new row keeps its top line and becomes the first of its row, nothing hanging on the outside, nothing doubled. Pseudo-elements and not borders, because the contract is that a cell in a band measures as frameless — `border-width` actually 0.

`balance` is **off by default here**, unlike `TGrid`, and that is a finding rather than an oversight: a stat's value scales to its own cell, so a lone cell stretched across the last row prints a number visibly larger than its siblings', and size on a dashboard reads as importance. The orphan beside empty space is the lesser mistake; the real fix for a band is a track count that divides the figures.

**`TStat` gains `metaPlacement`.** `bottom` puts the note under the value — the reading order of an indicator with a footnote, and a height fix: on the label's line a long note wraps onto a line of its own *above* the value, that one tile grows, and the grid then stretches the whole row so every sibling shows an empty band. Measured on a row of four with notes of different lengths: 115px each and every value top within a pixel, against one tile at 120 and three at 93 before. The DOM follows the visual order, so the announced order becomes label, value, note. Below 16rem of tile the trend chip also drops to its own line rather than sitting on the value — `flex-wrap` alone does not do it, because the chip is `flex: 0 0 auto` and the value shrinks under it instead.
